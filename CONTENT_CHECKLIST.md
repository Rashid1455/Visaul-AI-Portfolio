# Portfolio Content Checklist

## Identity
- [ ] Professional name
- [ ] One-line positioning statement
- [ ] Profile photo or brand mark
- [ ] Short bio
- [ ] Location/time zone if useful
- [ ] Email
- [ ] Upwork/profile link
- [ ] LinkedIn/Instagram/Behance links if applicable

## Hero
Recommended:
**AI Image & Video Generation | Creative AI Visuals**

Supporting line:
Create cinematic AI photography, product visuals, social ads and short-form video with a design-led workflow.

## Projects
For every project, prepare:
- Cover image
- 5–10 supporting images or frames
- Short problem/brief
- Your role
- Tools/workflow
- Before/after where useful
- Prompt/process screenshots where useful
- Final deliverables
- Verified results

## Trust
- Testimonials only with permission
- Client logos only with permission
- Awards/certifications only when genuine
- Metrics only when documented

## Legal
- Confirm commercial rights for assets
- Avoid implying a client relationship when the project is personal/concept work
- Label AI-generated work where disclosure is required
- Do not publish confidential client material
